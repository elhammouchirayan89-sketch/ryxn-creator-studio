Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website of RYXN CREATOR STUDIO, a creator brand that publishes humor, facts and AI-based experiments on TikTok under the account @story.teller.ca.ht. The website presents the brand and links to that account.

2. Data we process

Technical access data. When you open a page, your browser sends technical information to the server that delivers it, such as your IP address, the time of the request, the requested page and browser details. Hosting providers commonly keep this data in log files for a limited time to deliver the website and keep it secure.

Messages. The website has no contact form, user accounts, comments or newsletter. If you contact us through TikTok, we process the data you send, such as your username and the content of your message, only to answer you.

We do not ask for sensitive information through the website, and we do not use analytics or advertising tools on it.

3. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we process technical access data based on our legitimate interest in presenting and securing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We process messages you send us to respond to your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

4. Cookies

The website does not set cookies of its own and contains no scripts for tracking or analytics. Your browser or the third-party services named below may still process technical data, such as your IP address, when they deliver content to you. Websites you reach through links, including TikTok, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

5. Third-party services

Hosting. The website is delivered by a hosting provider, which processes the technical access data described above on our behalf.

Google Fonts. The pages load the fonts DM Sans, JetBrains Mono and Unbounded from Google Fonts. When a page is opened, your browser connects to servers operated by Google, which receive at least your IP address and technical request data to deliver the font files.

TikTok. The website links to our TikTok account. It does not embed TikTok videos or plugins. If you follow a link, you leave this website and TikTok processes your data under its own privacy policy, which we cannot influence. Please read it on the TikTok website.

6. Data retention and transfers

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires. Messages are kept until your request is resolved. Some providers named above may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, it should rely on a recognized mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorized access, including delivery of the website over an encrypted connection where available. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, and in particular under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and, where the legal requirements are met, have data erased or its processing restricted;
  • receive data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

The website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, the services it uses or the law change. The current version is always on this page, and the date at the top shows the last update.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, send a direct message to our TikTok account @story.teller.ca.ht.
